Based in Toronto · Matching across Canada

How it works

Three steps, all in writing, no surprises.

1

A homeowner posts a job

Someone needs a clean, a repair, a move, snow clearing — they post it, or we spot the post and offer to help. Connecting a homeowner is always free.

2

Local pros quote it

We ask local pros in that trade and area for a real price and a real time window. No obligation, no pressure — a number the homeowner can say yes or no to.

3

We connect you directly

Once both sides agree on price and timing, we introduce you in the same conversation. From there you deal with each other directly — scheduling, access, payment, everything.

What it costs

This is the part people ask about first, so here it is in plain terms.

For homeowners
$0 — always

You never pay Tukk Reviews anything. You pay your pro directly, for the work, after you've agreed the price with them.

For pros — first connection
Free

Your first introduction through us is free. We'd rather prove the service works than charge you for a promise.

For pros — after that
$20 flat

From your second connection on, it's a flat $20 per introduction, paid before contact details are exchanged. That's the only money that ever passes through us — job payments always go straight from the homeowner to you.

No percentages. No subscriptions. No lead auctions. If a job falls through through no fault of yours, the $20 becomes a credit toward your next connection, or a refund on request.

How we work, so you can check us

  • Everything in writing. We coordinate by message and email — we don't do coordination by phone. Written quotes and written agreements protect both sides.
  • Real prices before anyone commits. A pro's quote goes to the homeowner exactly as given. The homeowner decides.
  • Details stay private until both sides say yes. No address or phone number is shared before a price and a time are agreed.
  • We publish real price data. Quotes we process feed the public GTA Home Cost Index — anonymized, with sample sizes shown.
  • Reach us any time: tukkreviews@gmail.com. We answer in the same thread we're already talking in.
