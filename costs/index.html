What moves the price

Patterns already visible in this small first sample — the same factors will show up in your quotes.

Travel distance

A provider outside your area may add a travel surcharge — one quote above added CA$180. Nearby pros usually quote tighter.

House size & scope

A 4-bed deep clean is a different job from a 2-hour small clean. Size, rooms, and condition drive the flat quotes.

Supplies included or not

One homeowner switched to CA$30/hr using their own supplies instead of a flat quote. Who supplies products changes the math.

How this index works

  1. Real quotes only. Every point comes from a quote or offer that actually passed through Tukk's GTA Home Help operations — never scraped listicles, never invented.
  2. What's recorded: the service, the city/area at neighbourhood level, the quoted price, the pricing unit (flat, per visit, or per hour), the date, and the outcome (accepted, declined, countered, or pending) when known.
  3. Anonymized, always. No names, no addresses, no identifiable job details. Ever.
  4. Quoted vs paid is labelled. Sample size (n) and the date range are always shown beside the data. With fewer than 5 points in a group, we show the individual points — never a fake "average."
  5. Kept current. Points older than 12 months retire from the headline data. The index is republished monthly with a changelog.
